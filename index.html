Regulations

Format explained

  • The first 10 matches are league stage format
  • A win is 3pts, a draw is 1pt, and a loss is 0
  • The four teams with the best results pass to the semifinals
  • If teams are drawn on points then:
    1. Goal difference
    2. Goals for
    3. Head-to-head result
    4. Fewest goals conceded
  • Standard elimination knockout format for semi-finals and final.

Rules

  • Big pitch lines
  • 9 players (+subs)
  • At least one player from each gender must play a minimum of 5 minutes
  • No metal studs
  • No slide tackles (if deemed violent: straight red card)
  • No offside rule
  • 20 min games (10 min each half, 1 min pause, all games start at 13:35)
  • If teams draw (in knockout stage):
    • Penalty shootout 3rd break
    • 3 penalty takers per team
    • If no winner, it will continue as a sudden death shootout
NO INSULT TO OFFICIALS OR TO OTHER PLAYERS WILL BE TOLERATED
